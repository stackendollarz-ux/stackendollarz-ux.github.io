Delete your Yard Sale account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Yard Sale and tap You at the bottom.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

By email

Can't open the app? Email stackendollarz@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Yard Sale account." We'll delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, listings, photos, Neighbors posts, questions and follows.
  • Your login.

What we keep

Order and payment records we are required to keep by law (for taxes, refunds and disputes), and records of bans for safety. Messages you sent stay visible to the other person in that chat.
