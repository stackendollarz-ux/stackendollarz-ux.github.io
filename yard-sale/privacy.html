Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy explains what the Yard Sale app ("the App", "we") collects, how we use it, and your choices. Questions: stackendollarz@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase and never seen by us). You confirm you are 18 or older.
  • Your city: the city you choose or, if you allow it, your approximate location (rounded to about half a mile) so we can show items near you. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Listings and photos: item photos, titles, descriptions, prices and the general pickup area you enter.
  • Messages and posts: private chats between buyers and sellers, public questions on listings, and Neighbors posts.
  • Curb alerts: if you post one, its photo and an approximate spot (about one block) are shown to nearby users for a few hours.
  • Orders: what you bought or sold, prices, shipping address for shipped orders, tracking numbers, reviews, offers and problem reports (including photos you attach).
  • Payments: card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Sellers give their identity and bank details directly to Stripe to get paid.
  • In-app purchases: "Remove ads" and extra posts are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Device and ads: a notification token (to send sale and order alerts), your device's advertising ID and basic device info used by Google AdMob to show ads, and crash/usage information.
  • AI listing help (optional): if you tap "Fill it in for me," the photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to suggest a title, description and price. It is not used to identify people.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show listings, process orders, hold and release payments, ship, refund and review.
  • To keep people safe: detect scams, review reports and problem claims, and enforce strikes and bans. If you report a conversation, our safety team can read it.
  • To send notifications you'd expect, like sales, offers, shipping reminders and followed sellers' new items.
  • To show ads (Google AdMob) and local sponsor spots. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase.
  • For taxes and legal duties, such as seller tax forms handled by Stripe.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Other users see what you post publicly (listings, your seller name, city, badges, reviews, questions and Neighbors posts). Buyers and sellers in an order see what they need to complete it, such as the shipping address.
  • Service providers that run the App: Google Firebase (accounts, database, storage, servers), Stripe (payments and payouts), Google AdMob (ads), Google Play and RevenueCat (in-app purchases), Expo (app updates and notifications) and Anthropic (optional AI listing help).
  • Law enforcement or others when required by law, or to protect users from fraud or harm.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, listings, photos and posts. We keep order and payment records as long as the law requires (for example, for taxes and disputes).

Your choices

  • Change your city, turn off location or notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account any time in the App (You → Delete account) or as described on our account deletion page.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing stackendollarz@gmail.com.

Children

Yard Sale is for adults 18 and older, because buyers and sellers may meet in person. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the date above. Big changes will also be shown in the App.
