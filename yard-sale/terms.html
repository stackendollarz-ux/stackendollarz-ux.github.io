Who can use Yard Sale

You must be 18 or older and able to make a legal agreement. One account per person.

Buying and selling

  • Sellers must describe items honestly, with real photos, and ship within 24 hours of a sale with a real tracking number.
  • Pay only through the App. Payments outside the App (cash apps, gift cards, wire transfers) have no buyer protection.
  • Buyers pay the item price, shipping (if shipped) and a small service fee shown at checkout.
  • Yard Sale holds the payment until the buyer confirms they have the item, or until 10 days after shipping (48 hours after pickup) pass with no problem reported. New sellers may have a longer hold.
  • If an item is fake, empty, broken or not as described, the buyer can report a problem before the seller is paid. We review photos, tracking and messages and may refund the buyer. We may ask for the item to be returned; the seller pays return shipping when the item wasn't as described.
