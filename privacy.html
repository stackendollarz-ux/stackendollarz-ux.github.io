Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This policy explains what the Stack a Dollar app (the "App") collects, how Stacked Dollar ("we") uses it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, and the payment details you give us for cash-outs.
  • Device details: device ID, advertising ID, IP address, app version, and approximate location.
  • Activity: games played, ads watched, offers completed, Coins earned, and cash-outs.
  • Referrals: who invited you and who you invited, so we can pay referral bonuses.

How we use it

  • To run the App, track and pay rewards, and prevent fraud.
  • To show ads and offers. Our partners include Google AdMob and our offerwall partners. They collect data under their own privacy policies.

Who we share it with

Your username and weekly Coin total appear on the public leaderboard inside the App. Ad networks and offer partners (to show ads and confirm rewards), payment providers such as PayPal and gift cards (to send cash-outs), our hosting provider Google Firebase, and authorities when the law requires. We do not sell your personal information for money.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. After you delete your account, we may keep payment and fraud records for up to 7 years for tax, legal, and fraud-prevention reasons.

Deleting your account

Delete your account in the App under Settings > Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page. We delete your data within 30 days, except the records described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your data, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email stackendollarz@gmail.com to use these rights. We will not treat you differently for using them.

Ads and consent

In regions that require it, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Children

The App is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will post updates to this policy here and in the App, and change the date at the top.
