Delete your Stack a Dollar account

App: Stack a Dollar · Developer: Stacked Dollar

You can delete your Stack a Dollar account and its data at any time. You do not need to reinstall the App.

Option 1: in the App

  1. Open Stack a Dollar and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You will be signed out right away.

Option 2: by email

  1. Email stackendollarz@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. Send it from the email address on your account, and include your username if you have one.
  3. We will confirm by email once your account is deleted.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile, and login.
  • Your Coin balance, game history, and activity records.
  • Your device and advertising IDs linked to your account.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Records of past cash-outs and payments, kept up to 7 years for tax and legal reasons.
  • Fraud-prevention records, kept up to 7 years.

Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request. Unpaid Coins and cash balances are lost when you delete your account, so cash out first if your balance is $2.00 or more.
